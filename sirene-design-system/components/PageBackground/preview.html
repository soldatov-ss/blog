Architecture · Part 2

Roles are seams, not walls

Every role system starts small: admin, editor, viewer. Then a customer needs an editor who can't publish, and a viewer who can export.

Each exception is a patch sewn on top. After a year, the seams show everywhere and nobody remembers which stitch holds what.

Permissions fix that by cutting the cloth differently: small named abilities, combined into roles only at the edges of the system.
