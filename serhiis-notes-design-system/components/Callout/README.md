# Callout
Aside inside an article for notes, tips, warnings and danger.

**Consumer provides:** a kind (`note | tip | warn | danger`) and one or two short sentences.

- Tinted ground, coloured text and an uppercase word label (NOTE, TIP, WARNING, CAREFUL) — the word is required; colour never carries meaning alone.
- Tip is lagoon teal, danger is crimson: they differ in hue family and lightness, not red vs green.
- `radius-md`, padding `space-16` `space-24`, at `measure`. No side border, no emoji.
- At most two per article.
