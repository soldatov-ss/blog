Permissions should describe what a user can do, not who they are. Once we accepted that, the isAdmin checks scattered across the codebase stopped looking like shortcuts and started looking like debt.

Why hardcoded roles break

Every new customer asked for "admin, but without billing". We answered with a new role each time — read the original RFC for how that went.

  • Roles multiplied faster than features.
  • Checks lived in three layers.
  • Nobody could answer "who can delete a project?"
A role is just a named bundle of permissions. Name the bundle last.
